# 🛡️ SilverGuard — Senior Cyber Defense & Multilingual Threat Shield

> **Dedicated to Elder Digital Dignity, Multilingual Cyber Safety, and Rapid Police Assistance.**

SilverGuard is an accessible, real-time cyber protection web application designed specifically for senior citizens and their caregivers. It features **autonomous fraud interception**, **10-language voice alerts (Hindi, Marathi, Telugu, Tamil, Bengali, Kannada, Gujarati, Punjabi, Malayalam, and English)**, **Nearest Cyber Police Station & 1930 Helpline Locator**, **Automatic Call Audio Recording**, and **FIR-Ready Police Complaint Dossier Generation**.

---

## 🌟 Key Features

### 1. 🌐 Full Regional Multilingual Translation & Voice Synthesis
- Instant full-site UI translation into **10 languages**:
  - **हिंदी (Hindi)**
  - **मराठी (Marathi)**
  - **తెలుగు (Telugu)**
  - **தமிழ் (Tamil)**
  - **ಕನ್ನಡ (Kannada)**
  - **বাংলা (Bengali)**
  - **ગુજરાતી (Gujarati)**
  - **ਪੰਜਾਬੀ (Punjabi)**
  - **മലയാളം (Malayalam)**
  - **English**
- Integrated Web Speech API synthesized vocal warnings in the elder's native tongue.

### 2. 🚨 Auto-Guard Background Sentinel
- Background autonomous fraud monitoring and real-time threat interception.
- Sounds immediate voice warnings and displays high-visibility flashing alerts when a scam call, text, or WhatsApp forward is detected.

### 3. 💬 Message & Link Scam Scanner
- Heuristic detection for:
  - **Electricity Power Disconnection threats**
  - **Bank KYC / Pan Card expiry panic**
  - **KBC / Lottery Winner frauds**
  - **Grandchild bail & hospital emergency traps**
  - **Fake Police / Digital Arrest coercion**
  - **Lookalike phishing domains (.xyz, .top, .cc, etc.)**

### 4. 📞 Live Phone Call Screener & Audio Proof Recorder
- Real-time speech transcription with instant threat phrase highlighting.
- **Automatic Audio Call Recording**: Captures audio proof as downloadable `.wav` / `.webm` files.
- **Live Microphone Proof Mode**: Record active microphone audio during suspicious phone calls.

### 5. 📍 Nearest Cyber Police Station & 1930 Helpline Directory
- **National Cybercrime Helpline 1930**: 1-click calling to the 24x7 Citizen Financial Cyber Fraud Reporting System.
- **GPS Distance Locator**: Automatically locates and sorts the closest Cyber Crime Police Stations (Mumbai BKC, Pune, Delhi IFSO, Bengaluru CID, Hyderabad, Chennai, Kolkata, Ahmedabad, Jaipur, Lucknow).
- **Golden Hour Protocol**: Step-by-step guidance to freeze unauthorized bank transactions within 2 hours.

### 6. 📁 Digital Evidence Locker & FIR Complaint Dossier
- Tamper-evident proof vault with **SHA-256 digital signature hashes**, caller IDs, and transcripts.
- **1-Click Police FIR Dossier Generator**: Generates formal, printable legal complaint letters ready for `cybercrime.gov.in` or local cyber police stations.

### 7. 🎯 "Spot The Scam" Interactive Quiz & Caregiver Bridge
- 5-question senior safety quiz with side-by-side comparison clues.
- Emergency SOS dispatch simulating WhatsApp and SMS alerts to caregivers.

## 🚀 Live Demo & Deployment (GitHub Pages)

This repository is built with vanilla HTML5, CSS3, and JavaScript (Zero external backend dependencies required, 100% Client-Side Privacy).

To host on **GitHub Pages**:
1. Go to your repository **Settings** on GitHub.
2. Under the **Code and automation** section, click on **Pages**.
3. Under **Build and deployment > Branch**, select `main` branch and `/ (root)` folder.
4. Click **Save**.
5. Your live link will be generated at:
   `https://chirantanpatil251-tech.github.io/SilverGuard-/`

## 🔒 Privacy & Data Security
- **100% Client-Side**: All scans, voice synthesis, audio recordings, and evidence storage remain securely on the user's local device (`localStorage`).
- No personal messages or banking data are uploaded to third-party tracking servers.
