SCAM MESSAGE

Fake Electricity Power Cut SMS

"Dear Consumer, your electricity power will be disconnected tonight at 9:30 PM because your previous bill was not updated. Immediately call Electricity Officer at 98234-XXXXX or download Mahavitaran APK."
✕ Urgency: Gives an immediate night deadline ("tonight at 9:30 PM").
✕ Shady Contact: Personal mobile number instead of official electricity portal.
✕ Malicious APK: Asks to install unknown APK app that steals banking OTPs.
GENUINE MESSAGE

Real Electricity Board SMS

"MSEDCL Alert: Bill for Consumer No. 01849203 for Rs. 1,420 is due on 25-Oct. Pay securely via official website https://www.mahadiscom.in or authorized payment centers. Disregard if already paid."
✓ Safe Verification: Quotes your registered consumer account number and exact bill amount.
✓ Official Domain: Links only to genuine .in government domain.
✓ No Threat: Normal due date with no sudden cutoff threats.
